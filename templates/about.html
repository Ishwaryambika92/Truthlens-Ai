METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE

Verify News. Understand the Truth.

TruthLens AI is an advanced misinformation detection system. We combine historical stylistic machine learning, real-time external web evidence verification, and accredited journalist fact-checking into a single, transparent verdict.

LAYER 1

Stylistic ML Classifier

Evaluates syntax, vocabulary distributions, and sensationalist rhetoric. Provides a stylistic probability signal, not proof of factual truth.

LAYER 2

Live Web Evidence

Extracts central claims and queries Google Fact Check Tools and real-time news reporting via Tavily to verify real-world occurrences.

LAYER 3

Temporal Era Anchoring

Distinguishes contemporary breaking events from historical archives so present-day developments are never penalized by older training sets.

LAYER 4

Evidence Override Verdict

Real-world corroborated reporting always supersedes stylistic ML predictions, outputting LIKELY REAL, LIKELY FAKE, MIXED / MISLEADING, or UNCERTAIN.
